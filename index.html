About the Artist

Meet Prem Tavare

Visual Storyteller & Cinematic Photographer

My name is Prem Tavare, and photography has been my passion since I was in the 5th standard. What started as a simple interest slowly became something I truly love and enjoy. From an early age, I found happiness in capturing moments, emotions, and stories through my camera.

As my journey grew, I had the opportunity to work under well-known and experienced photographers, learning valuable skills, techniques, and creative vision from them. I enjoy shooting all types of photography, as every shoot brings a new experience and a new story to tell. Photography allows me to express myself and connect deeply with the moments I capture..

For me, photography is more than a profession—it gives me peace and balance. Music is also a big part of my creative process; I love listening to music while working, as it helps me stay calm and inspired. Through my lens, I aim to create meaningful images that preserve emotions and memories forever.

My Fashion Philosophy

Photography, to me, is the art of preserving authentic emotions and meaningful moments. My primary focus is on weddings and pre-weddings, where genuine connections, unscripted expressions, and timeless rituals come together to tell powerful stories. I approach every shoot with sensitivity and attention to detail, ensuring each frame reflects the true essence of the moment rather than staged perfection.

Alongside people-centric storytelling, I draw inspiration from nature and wildlife, finding calm and balance in their raw beauty. I also specialize in creating cinematic films for weddings and events, where visuals, music, and movement combine to create emotionally engaging narratives. Through both photography and filmmaking, my goal is to deliver work that feels refined, sincere, and enduring.
